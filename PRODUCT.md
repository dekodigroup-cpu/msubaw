# PRODUCT.md

## Register

`brand` — this is a marketing and identity site. The design *is* the product.

## What this is

msubaw.com is the public home of **MSU Black Alumni**, the standing community of
Black graduates of Mississippi State University. Its best-known programme is
**Black Alumni Weekend (BAW)**, a four-day reunion held every two years in
February in Starkville, Mississippi.

The site does three jobs, in priority order:

1. Convince an alumnus to give to the scholarship fund, or sponsor an event.
2. Answer "when is the next one and should I come" without friction.
3. Hold the record: galleries and history from each weekend.

## Users

**Primary: the returning alumnus.** 30 to 75, lives outside Mississippi, often
in Atlanta, Memphis, Dallas, Houston or Chicago. Reads on a phone. Decides two
things: whether to travel in February, and whether to write a cheque. Has
limited patience for a site that buries either answer.

**Secondary: the sponsor.** A company or individual evaluating whether the
weekend reaches an audience worth paying for. Needs the programme, the venues
and a contact, fast.

**Tertiary: the current student.** Looking for the scholarship and the career
mixer. Youngest and most mobile-dependent visitor.

## Brand and tone

Confident, warm, plainspoken. The subject is sixty years of Black achievement at
an institution that did not always welcome it, so the register is
**commemorative, not promotional**. No hype, no exclamation marks, no
institutional throat-clearing.

Write like a well-made printed programme: short declaratives, concrete nouns,
real numbers. Say "every ticket becomes tuition", not "we are committed to
fostering excellence".

## Strategic principles

- **Never invent an event date.** BAW28 dates are unannounced. The site says
  "February 2028, dates to be announced" and nothing more specific. People book
  flights off this page.
- **Scholarship first.** The money is the point of the weekend. Giving is never
  more than one click from any page.
- **The archive is an asset.** Three documented weekends is proof the thing is
  real and recurring. Keep every year reachable.
- **Honest placeholders over fakes.** Where photographs are missing, hand off to
  the real gallery. Never ship decorative tiles pretending to be photos.

## Anti-references

Things this site must not resemble:

- **Crest-and-stock-photo university site.** Seal in the corner, smiling
  students on a quad, navy gradient. The first-order category reflex.
- **Afrocentric-template reflex.** Kente borders, gold-on-black, drum
  iconography applied as decoration rather than meaning.
- **SaaS landing page.** Hero metric row, three identical feature cards,
  purple gradient, "Get started free".
- **Giant-serif editorial portfolio.** The second-order reflex once the first
  two are avoided. Oversized display serif on cream, nothing else.
